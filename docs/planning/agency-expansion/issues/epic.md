## Problem

Tanaghom's first proof of concept covers sales, content, and marketing, but the approved product vision is an AI organization spanning all departments. The 273-profile Agency Agents snapshot is a source of candidate methods, not 273 operational Tanaghom workers. Chat-only decisions and stale issue descriptions make developer handoff unreliable.

## User story

As a business owner, I want coordinated specialist teams with clear ownership, capabilities, evidence, and human controls, starting with the marketing/content/sales pilot and expanding across departments.

## Scope

- Preserve all 273 profiles in the pinned 18-division inventory and map every profile to a delivery owner issue.
- Deliver the six-profile pilot, comparative evaluation, bounded team coordination, team operations experience, and governed learning as separately accepted slices.
- Reuse #131/#135/#137 and existing #150-#157 tracks rather than create parallel Hermes, analytics, content, or connector systems.
- Track every future department through explicit waves; catalog coverage is not activation coverage.
- Maintain a versioned status/handoff, decision record, issue-body snapshots, dependency map, and source provenance in this repository.
