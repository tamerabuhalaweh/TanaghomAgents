# Creative segmentation evaluation v1 (acceptance set, not a quality certificate)

Status: LEDGER ONLY until bounded human visual review signs masks and
cutouts. Fixture runs prove pipeline mechanics, determinism, and RGB
preservation; they do not certify real-photo quality.

## Corpus

`corpus.json` pins 12 categories. Fixtures are generated by
`scripts/creative-segmentation-fixtures.mjs` (deterministic, seeded)
into `evaluation/creative-segmentation-v1/fixtures/`. Packaging
fixtures use band geometry (no real glyphs offline); text safety is
proven structurally — segmentation only ever rewrites the alpha
channel, so RGB (logos, text, colors) is preserved by construction
and asserted in unit tests.

## What runs without credentials

- Fixture segmentations through the local engine with ledger lines:
  `{case_id, coverage, bytes, latency_ms, result}`.
- One real BiRefNet CPU inference on a synthetic product photo is
  recorded separately (see the decision record): load 5.2s, infer
  72.1s @1024², clean cutout. BiRefNet weights are never bundled.
- E2E upload → segment → Product Studio scene → fidelity review →
  approval, all with real PNG bytes.

## Human gates

Masks and cutouts require human visual sign-off per the recorded
columns (edge quality, missing/leaked regions, text preservation)
before any virtual-studio parity claim. This slice claims pipeline
completion, not parity.
