## Before a word (per piece)
Who reads + what they believe; one thing they must understand; one action; real objection (not polite version). No CTA → do not write.

## Writing
- Lead with consequence, not mechanism. Feature = evidence, not claim.
- Specific > superlative. "3 days → 20 min" beats "dramatically faster". Every superlative competitor also claims → cut.
- Reader's words (research/tickets), not internal vocab.
- One idea per piece/section, reader order: recognized problem → why current options fail → what you do differently → proof → next step.
- Every claim carries proof nearby (number/name/screenshot/quote).

## Hook (social/reel, decides everything)
- Winners: specific claim+number; stated tension; named mistake reader makes; outcome before method.
- Losers: yes/no question, definition, throat-clearing, "In today's world".
- Write hook LAST. One idea per post; hook + turn (why obvious view incomplete) + substance (specific/first-hand) + landing (conclusion, not engagement-beg).

## Formatting (not cosmetic)
- Visible fold = 1–3 lines decides read. First line must stand alone.
- Short paragraphs, one thought per line, no walls. Structure carries emphasis (platforms strip formatting).
- Never reuse one format unchanged across platforms (breaks/fold/links differ). Decide link handling per platform (suppression risk).

## Edit passes (in order)
1. Structure (order/duplication) 2) Cut (every non-advancing sentence + first paragraph warm-up) 3) Sharpen (specifics, active, claims) 4) Read aloud — stumble = rewrite.

## Pre-publish check (Agent 2 self-check before queue)
First line strong alone? Survives fold? One idea? Specific to you (not anyone-in-field)? Would you send to one respected person? Fail → rewrite, never queue with caveat. Draft lives where it can be reviewed before scheduler; never edit inside publish queue.
